Fun Run Schedule

Thursday, May 21 — Timeline subject to change. Wear comfy shoes and dress for the weather!

12:45 – 1:00

Students receive event t-shirts by grade (different colors). Line up by class and walk to the Cashmere High School track escorted by the Cashmere Fire Department and marching band. Community members are invited to line Pioneer Ave to cheer.

1:00 – 1:45

Fun Run begins! Students walk/run for approximately 30–45 minutes with water stations and restrooms available. Families are welcome to cheer or participate.

1:45 – 2:00

Students line up with teachers and walk back to Vale, running through water sprayed by the fire truck along the way. Crunch Pak provides snacks. Parents may pick up children at the office.
